What a skill looks like

listing-copy owns title, subtitle, first eight lines, what’s inside, specs, license. Good means the first screen answers what it is, what’s in the file, the price, and the license. It refuses fake testimonials and fake sales counts.

description: Write a Gumroad/itch listing that a cold visitor can buy from.

# Listing copy
You are writing a product page, not a blog post.

## Own
- Title, subtitle, first 8 lines, what's inside

## Never without asking
- Fake testimonials, fake sales counts

That’s the pattern: a job, a bar, a refusal. The other eleven follow it.
