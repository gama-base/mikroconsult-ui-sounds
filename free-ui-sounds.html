Free UI sound effects, as WAV

I needed a click for a prototype and every “free UI sounds” zip I found was either MP3, a cinematic whoosh, or a 400-file library I would never finish browsing.

So I made twelve short WAV files and left them on Gumroad for $0.

What’s in the zip

  • click_primary, click_secondary, tap_soft
  • hover, select
  • toggle_on, toggle_off
  • success, error, notify
  • whoosh_open, whoosh_close

Format: WAV, 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit. Most sit under 150 ms. Clean sine tones — not recuts of someone else’s pack.

Four of them, here

Same files as in the free zip. The other eight are in the download.

How I drop them in

  • Web: new Audio('/sounds/click_primary.wav').play()
  • Figma: attach as interaction audio on the component. Step-by-step: Figma prototype sounds
  • Godot: one-shot on the Button. Step-by-step: Godot UI sounds
  • Unity: PlayOneShot on the Button. Step-by-step: Unity UI sounds
