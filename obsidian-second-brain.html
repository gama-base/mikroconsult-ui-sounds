Search “obsidian second brain template free” and you get a vault with twelve empty databases and a YouTube series.

I wanted a folder I could open on Monday and still use on Friday. Solo OS Lite is that folder. PARA, a daily note that takes two minutes, and a weekly review whose only job is to name one thing that ships.

What you actually get

  • para/ — projects, areas, resources, archive. Four markdown files, not a plugin zoo.

  • templates/weekly.md — shipped, next week’s ship, kill/pause
  • CSV stubs in /import if you ever want Notion

Open the folder as an Obsidian vault, or just open it in VS Code, Cursor, iA Writer. It’s markdown. No login.
