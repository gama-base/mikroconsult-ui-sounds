Figma prototype sound effects, as WAV

A silent Figma file looks finished and feels dead. These twelve clicks are short enough to hang on a component without covering the next screen.

They are original sine tones, not recuts. Figma’s Prototype Play sound action accepts WAV. Pay $0 for the zip.

Hear three of them

Drop a click on a Figma button

  1. Download the free pack. Unzip. You want click_primary.wav (85 ms) and maybe hover.wav (65 ms).
  2. Select the button. Open the Prototype tab.
  3. Add an interaction: On click → Play sound. Upload the WAV. Figma also takes MP3 and M4A if a teammate prefers those.
  4. Present the frame. The sound plays with the tap. Keep the file short so it does not bleed into the destination screen.

Play sound is a prototype action. If your plan does not show it, the same WAV still works in code: new Audio('/sounds/click_primary.wav').play().

Which file on which control

  • Primary button → click_primary.wav
  • Secondary / ghost → click_secondary.wav or tap_soft.wav
  • Hover (if you prototype pointer) → hover.wav
  • Toggle → toggle_on.wav / toggle_off.wav
  • Form ok / fail → success.wav / error.wav
  • Sheet open / close → whoosh_open.wav / whoosh_close.wav

Format: WAV · 44.1 kHz · mono · 16-bit. Most under 150 ms. Personal and commercial product use is fine. Don’t resell the zip as another sound pack.
